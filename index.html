FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is FlowDesk really free forever?+

Yes. The Free plan supports teams of up to 5 members with unlimited tasks and docs, and no credit card. Upgrade only when you need more seats or advanced features.

Can I migrate from Jira, Linear or Asana?+

Absolutely. We have one-click importers for Jira, Linear, Asana and Trello that bring over tasks, statuses, comments and attachments. Most teams are fully moved within an hour.

How does the AI sprint summary work?+

It reads activity across your sprint — task movements, comments and PR links — and writes a short status update: what shipped, what's blocked and what's at risk. Your data is never used to train models.

Is my data secure?+

FlowDesk is SOC 2 Type II compliant, with SAML SSO, audit logs and encryption in transit and at rest. Team-plan customers can choose regional data residency.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes — there are no lock-in contracts. Cancel from your billing settings at any time and you'll keep access until the end of your current period.
